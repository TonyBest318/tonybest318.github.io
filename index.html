Tiancheng (Tony) Wu

I am a MS in Robotics student at the CMU Robotics Institute. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zackory Erickson, and I was also privileged to work closely with Prof. David Held. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Tongji University, where I was honored to be advised by Prof. Kai Yang.

I aspire to become a full-stack roboticist developing generalist robots that operate seamlessly in everyday human environments.
